Background

A little about me →

Hi! I'm Minji Park, a senior double-majoring in Art & Technology and Computer Science at Sogang University, working at the intersection of design and data. I started my journey in design, then got pulled toward data, now I build things that use pattern recognition and sentiment analysis. While I'm still learning with machine learning and training data, I care more about visible progress rather than the polished final product, which is why I working with people I can bounce ideas off in real time. Growing up between Thailand and Korea, I've learned to asking how a design choice lands for someone outside my own frame of reference, it shows up in how I build, not just how I talk about myself. I like working in the open with a team, shipping in visible pieces rather than disappearing until a "final" version. Other than that, during free time I usually go for running, and have coffee and talk with friends.
